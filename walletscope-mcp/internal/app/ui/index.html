WalletScope

Ethereum wallet investigation Checking…
Paste an Ethereum address and press Investigate.
WalletScope will profile it, list recent activity, screen it against OFAC, trace outgoing funds and build an explainable risk report.

Results are investigative indicators from public data (Blockscout, OFAC SDN). A sanctions screening hit is not a legal determination, “no match” is not compliance clearance, and risk scores require analyst review.
